Materials

Four polymers we mould every week.

Tell us what the part has to survive (load, heat, chemicals, sunlight) and we will suggest the grade.

PP

Polypropylene

Light, tough and chemical-resistant. Takes living hinges well.

Typical partsCaps, closures, containers, battery and appliance parts
ABS

ABS

Rigid with a clean surface finish. Good for visible parts.

Typical partsHousings, switch and electrical covers, panels, knobs
HDPE

High-density polyethylene

Impact-resistant and food-contact friendly grades available.

Typical partsCrates, bins, household ware, pipe and tank fittings
PA

Nylon (PA6 / PA66)

Strong and wear-resistant. Glass-filled grades for load-bearing parts.

Typical partsGears, bushes, clips, cable ties, automotive brackets

How an order runs

From drawing to dispatch in five steps.

  1. 01

    Send the drawing or sample

    PDF, DWG, STEP or a photo of the part. Add material, quantity and colour.

  2. 02

    Quote and mould plan

    We quote the piece rate, and the mould cost if a new mould is needed.

  3. 03

    Trial shots, sample approval

    First-off parts go to you for fit and finish. Production starts only after your OK.

  4. 04

    Production run

    Moulded on our machines in Vasai to the approved sample, batch by batch.

  5. 05

    Inspect, pack, dispatch

    Checked, counted and packed the way your stores want it, with GST invoice.
