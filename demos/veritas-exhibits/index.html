Services

Everything between the floor plan and opening day.

One contact for design, build, branding and the crew on site. No juggling a carpenter, a printer and an electrician the night before the show.

  1. 01

    Custom stalls

    Built from scratch around your products and floor plan, in wood, laminate, acrylic, glass and backlit fabric.

  2. 02

    Modular & Octanorm stalls

    Aluminium system stalls that go up fast, look clean and can be reused across shows.

  3. 03

    3D stall design

    Renders at real dimensions, so you approve the stall before a single panel is cut.

  4. 04

    Fabrication

    Carpentry, paint finishes, graphics and lighting, built and pre-fitted off-site.

  5. 05

    On-site installation

    Our crew builds during move-in, sets up power and lighting, and dismantles after the show.

  6. 06

    Event branding

    Fascia, backlit panels, LED walls, standees and counters that match your brand guide.

Process

From brief to show day in eight weeks.

A typical custom stall, counted back from the day the hall opens. Modular stalls can move faster; ask us what your dates allow.

  1. Week 8

    Brief & floor plan

    Expo, stall number, size, open sides and what you are showing.

  2. Weeks 7–6

    3D design

    Concept renders, your changes, then a clear quote with GST.

  3. Week 5

    Organiser approval

    Stall drawings prepared and submitted to the organiser on time.

  4. Weeks 4–2

    Fabrication

    Structure, finishes and graphics built and test-fitted off-site.

  5. Move-in

    Installation

    Build, lighting, branding and a walk-through before the hall opens.

  6. After the show

    Dismantle

    Crew strips the stall and clears your space within the organiser's window.
