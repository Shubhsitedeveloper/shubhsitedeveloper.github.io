How it works

Three steps to a clean home.

  1. Book a slot

    Pick the package, date and time slot online, whenever it suits you. You get a confirmation on WhatsApp.

  2. The team arrives

    A trained team reaches your door in the slot you chose, with its own machines and cleaning materials.

  3. Checklist clean

    Every room is worked through a written checklist. You walk through with the team before they leave.

Kitchen checklist 4 / 6

  • Wall tiles degreased
  • Hob & chimney outside
  • Platform & sink
  • Cabinets inside
  • Floor scrub & mop
  • Walk-through with you

Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to keep anything ready?

Only water and electricity. The team brings its own machines, cloths and cleaning solutions. Please empty kitchen cabinets if you have booked them to be cleaned inside. [Owner to confirm]

How long does a 2BHK deep clean take?

Most of a day, depending on the flat's condition. We confirm the expected time on WhatsApp after you book. [Owner to confirm]

Can I change or cancel my slot?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we will move the booking to another free slot. [Your cancellation policy]

How do I pay?
