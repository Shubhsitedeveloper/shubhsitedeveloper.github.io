Material

Why we build in SS 304, not 202

Both look the same on delivery day. The difference shows a year later, after salt, tamarind, steam and daily cleaning chemicals.

GradeSS 304OURSSS 202
NickelAbout 8%About 4–6%
Rust & pittingHolds up to salt, acids, wet floorsCan spot and pit in wet areas
Food contactStandard food-grade choiceBetter kept for dry, low-wear use
Best forTops, sinks, hoods, rangesBudget frames & dry shelving

Ask for the grade

Ask any supplier to write the steel grade and sheet gauge on the quote.

Clean welds

Ground and finished joints leave no gaps for grease to collect.

Thicker where it matters

Heavier sheet on tops and range frames that take daily load.

Complete kitchen setup

One team from empty room to first order

Opening a new outlet or redoing an old kitchen? We plan the layout, make every steel piece and fit it on site — so the hood lines up with the range and the sink drains where the plumber left the pipe.

  1. 01 · SITE VISIT

    Measure the site

    We visit, measure walls, gas, water and drain points, and note your menu and covers.

  2. 02 · LAYOUT

    Draw the layout

    A layout with each station — receiving, prep, cooking, wash, pass — and a line-item quote in ₹.

  3. 03 · FABRICATION

    Build in our workshop

    Cut, bent and welded at Gala A-5, DPK Compound. You can visit and check the work before delivery.

  4. 04 · INSTALLATION

    Install & hand over

    Delivered, levelled and fixed on site, with hood ducting connected and every piece checked with you.
