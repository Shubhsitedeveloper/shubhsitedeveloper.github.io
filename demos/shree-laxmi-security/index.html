How we work

What your committee gets with every contract.

The checks and paperwork societies and offices ask for, handled by us before day one.

Police verification

Every guard and cleaner goes through police verification before deployment. Copies on file for your records.

Uniform and ID card

Supervisor on rounds

Replacement policy

Monthly paperwork
